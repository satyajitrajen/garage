# Voice-over script

Record one take per video while watching its silent version (start at 0:00).
Save it in `voiceover/` named after the video, e.g. `00-getting-started.wav` (.m4a/.mp3 work too), then rebuild.
Words = how many fit before the next line at ~2.6 words/s; a little over is fine.

Combined video only (`00-intro.wav`, over the 5s opening card):
